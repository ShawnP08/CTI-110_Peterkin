# Xerox The Sandwich Making Robot

## Assumptions
- We bought the pack of brioche bread 2 days ago so it's still fairly fresh.
- We just took 2 slices out of the pack but untwisting the the little white plastic thing.
- In our hand we have a sliver spoon we took out the drawer with a nice spoonful of mayo to put on both sides of the bread.
- On the table we have pepper jack cheese, salsalito turkey thats been warmed up, lettuce, tomato, and mustard.


## Action Phase
1. We realize we forgot to toast to bread so put the spoonful of mayo down on the top of the mayo and we pick up the toaster that is sitting inside the pantry and toast the bread on the 3 and 1/2 setting for about 2 minutes.
2. We take the bread out of the toaster by lifting up the silver piece, set it on the table, then put the mayo on both sides of the bread.
3. We pick up the mustard and get a nice squeeze of it on the bread.
4. We unzip the package of cheese ripping apart the cheese as we take it from the layer of parchement paper.
5. We put a piece of cheese ontop of both pieces of bread. 
6. We then put the hot salsalito turkey ontop of the cheese so that it can melt.
7. After we set the lettuce and tomato in that order ontop of the turkey.
8. Lastly we close the sandwich together then cut it in half diagonally to have 2 triangle cuts of our beautiful sandwich.

## Done When
- The 2 pieces of bread are put together then cut into 2 triangluar pieces.
